Encrypt the case studies

This page runs only on your computer and can’t connect to the internet. Your password never leaves this window.

1Choose your site-source folder

It’s inside your portfolio repo. Your browser may ask to confirm reading the folder; nothing is uploaded.

2Enter the password

Use 4 or more random words or at least 16 characters. Visitors type this to open the case studies.

3Encrypt and save

Save straight into your repo’s docs folder (Chrome or Edge), or download a .zip and unzip it into docs.

  • Then commit and push docs. The readable site-source folder stays on your computer.
  • To change the password later, run this again with the new one and push.
  • Encrypting again turns off any access links you’ve shared. Make new ones below.

4Make an access link optional

A link that opens the case studies without the password, for a recruiter or an application. Anyone with the link can get in, so share it like you would the password. It keeps working until you encrypt again.
